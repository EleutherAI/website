Advances in science and technology belong to the world, not just to those who can afford to develop them. Your donation supports open AI research and the people doing it: the models and tools they use, the questions they investigate, and the compute and mentorship that make their work possible.

## Research others can build on

Training a language model is out of reach for many research groups. Studying one should not be. We release models alongside the materials researchers need to understand them, including training data, code, and checkpoints from throughout training.

[Pythia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01373) makes it possible to study how models learn under controlled conditions. [GPT-NeoX](https://github.com/EleutherAI/gpt-neox) gives researchers the infrastructure to train models of their own. Maintaining and sharing these resources lets independent teams pursue questions they could not otherwise investigate.

> "While other organizations have released open models, nobody designs their releases to empower scientific research better than EleutherAI. My lab fundamentally relies on access to things like training data and partially trained models to pursue our agenda of understanding why and how LLMs work. In an ideal world we would be able to train our own bespoke models from scratch, but seeing as how we can't, I consider access to models like EleutherAI's to be as essential to our current research as having access to GPUs and PhD students. That is: absolutely essential."
>
> **Ellie Pavlick, Brown University**

## A way into research

Access to a model is only part of what a researcher needs. Through our community and the Summer of Open AI Research (SOAR), we connect aspiring researchers with mentorship, collaborators, and computing resources. Your support helps people develop the skills and experience to lead work of their own.

> "SOAR gave me the opportunity to get my foot in the door and actually contribute to a publication-worthy AI research project. It connected me with talented mentors who helped build the foundations for my research journey."
>
> **Ayesha I., SOAR mentee**

## Independent questions, public research

We study how AI systems learn, how to evaluate them, and how to make open models safer. Our research agenda is chosen independently. We do not accept directed funding for work we did not already want to do.

Almost all of our work develops in public, in our Discord community. Researchers can follow projects as they take shape, contribute to them, and build on the results. Philanthropic support helps sustain this way of working, including work that has scientific value without an immediate commercial application.

> "When I look at EleutherAI and all that you're doing, I see a young Mozilla Foundation."
>
> **Mark Surman, President of the Mozilla Foundation**

Read our [2025 annual report (PDF)](/reports/eleutherai-2025-annual-report.pdf) for more about our research, community, and finances.
